# Validation status

## Current evidence

The public evidence consists of CST antenna/balun models, simulated results, a PDF report, and fabrication ZIP files. No calibrated fabricated-assembly measurement is included.

## Required before a measured-performance claim

1. Freeze the exact source revision and fabrication package.
2. Record material/stack-up, component tolerances, solver settings, and calibration method.
3. Fabricate representative samples.
4. Measure with calibrated equipment and publish uncertainty.
5. Compare analytical, simulated, and measured results without replacing failed cases.
6. Record thermal, power, stability, and regulatory constraints where applicable.
